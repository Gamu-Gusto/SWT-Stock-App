# User guide

Open **https://stock.superwheeltyres.co.za** on your phone or computer and sign in with the e-mail address
and password the owner gave you. The first time, you must choose your own password.

**Install it on your phone:** in Chrome on Android open the menu ⋮ → *Add to Home screen* / *Install app*.
It then opens like an app. You still need internet – stock is always checked live and nothing is saved offline.

## "Do you have 205/55 R16?"

1. Tap **Search** (bottom bar on a phone) or type in the search box at the top.
2. Type the size any way you like: `205/55 R16`, `205/55R16`, `205 55 16`, or just `R16`.
   Add a brand or *used*: `Falken 205/55 R16`, `used 205/55 R16`. A tyre code like `UT-2026-000012` or a SKU also works.
3. Every matching tyre appears – all brands, new and used – with:
   - **price per tyre** (used tyres show a price range – each has its own price),
   - **physical** (in the building), **reserved** (held for customers) and **available** (can be sold now),
   - where it is stored, and the status *In stock / Low stock / Out of stock*.
4. From a result you can **Reserve**, **Sell** or **Quote** straight away, or open it for details and photos.

Only promise what is in **Available**. Reserved tyres belong to another customer until the reservation ends.

## Selling tyres

1. **Sell** (bottom bar) or *Sales → New sale*.
2. Search and add the tyres. For used tyres, choose the exact tyre (tread depth, grade and price are shown).
3. Set quantity, price and any discount. If you go below the minimum approved price, a manager must do the sale.
4. Add services with one tap: fitting, balancing, wheel alignment, puncture repair, valve, other.
5. Optionally enter the customer and vehicle.
6. Choose **Cash, Card or EFT**, then **Complete sale**. Stock is deducted once, at that moment.
   If someone else sold the last tyre a second earlier, you get a clear "only X available" message and nothing is saved.
7. Print the sale if the customer wants a copy. *Save as draft* keeps a sale without touching stock.

Tapping *Complete* twice (or a slow connection retrying) never records the sale twice.

## Reserving tyres

*Reservations → Create reservation* (or *Reserve* on a search result): add tyres, customer name and phone,
how long to hold them (default 24 hours). The tyres stay on the rack but cannot be sold to anyone else.
When the customer arrives open the reservation → **Sell reserved tyres**. You can extend, cancel or WhatsApp the
customer from the reservation. Unclaimed reservations release automatically when they expire.

## Quotations

*Quotations → New quotation*: add tyres and services; the total includes VAT if the business is registered.
**Send on WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the quote ready to send to the customer's number. **Print / PDF** gives a
branded printout. A quote never holds stock – convert it to a **reservation** or **sale** when the customer agrees.

## Receiving a delivery (stock staff)

1. *Add stock → Receive a delivery*.
2. Choose the supplier, enter the invoice or delivery-note number and the date.
3. Search each tyre that arrived, choose where it is stored and the quantity and cost. For used tyres enter each tyre
   (tread, grade, DOT, price, inspection result).
4. **Review and post**. The review shows the stock after posting. Posting updates stock once; drafts can be saved
   and finished later. Attach a photo of the invoice on the receipt page.
5. If the invoice number was already received, the system warns you – only post again if it really is a different delivery.

A tyre that is not in the system yet: *Add stock → Add a new tyre record* first (or ask a manager).

## Counting stock (stocktake)

1. Open **Count** (bottom bar) → choose the stocktake assigned to you.
2. Go rack by rack. Tap a line, enter what you **physically see** (use + / −), **Save count**.
   For used tyres tap **Found** or **Not found**.
3. Found something that is not on the list? **Found something not on the list** – scan/enter the used-tyre code or
   search the tyre, choose the location, enter the quantity.
4. The expected number is usually hidden – count what is really there.
5. When everything is counted, **Submit count**. A manager reviews the differences; stock only changes when they approve.

While a count is in progress, sales of the tyres being counted may be paused – that is deliberate, so the count is accurate.

## Moving and correcting stock

- **Transfer** (Stock movements → Transfers): from one rack/area to another. Both sides update together.
- **Adjustment** (Stock movements → Adjustments): correct a wrong quantity, mark tyres as damaged, write off, or return
  to a supplier. Always give a clear reason. A different manager approves it before stock changes.

## Notifications

The bell shows alerts for you: low or out-of-stock tyres, reservations about to expire, counts assigned to you and
items waiting for approval. Alerts disappear when the situation is fixed.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You are offline"** – reconnect; nothing was saved. Try again.
- **"Session expired"** – sign in again; you are taken back to where you were.
- **"Insufficient stock"** – someone sold or reserved those tyres; search again for the current figures.
- **"Locked by stocktake"** – those tyres are being counted; wait until the count is submitted or ask a manager.
- Forgot your password – use *Forgot your password?* on the sign-in page, or ask the owner for a temporary password.
